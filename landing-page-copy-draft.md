**U.S. Domestic Rare Earth Magnet Production Dashboard**

Tracking the companies rebuilding America's rare earth magnet supply chain — from mine to finished magnet.

China controls roughly 90% of global rare earth magnet production. This dashboard follows the 17 U.S. companies working to change that: miners, separators, metallizers, alloy producers, and magnet manufacturers, spanning everything from early-stage pilot lines to full commercial production.

Each profile is built from primary sources — SEC filings, DoD and DPA contract awards, company press releases, and earnings calls — and updated as significant developments occur. No modeling, no forecasts dressed up as facts: just what's been publicly disclosed, organized so you can see where the industry actually stands.
